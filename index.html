Stop wasting water !

Did you know out of all the water on the earth only about 0.3% is actually drinkable. Yes out off all the water on the earth 97.5% is salty. Of the remaining 2.5% 2.2% is frozen in ice sheets and glaciers meaning only 0.3% is drinkable for humans.

Water is needed for all living things to survive. Without it they will die and decay. This is famously shown in a quote Dr. Sylvia Earle, a famous oceanographer once said: "No water, no life. No blue, no green." She says this to remind us not a single thing can survive without water. Some can hold out very long but will still eventually die. She also means without blue (water) there cannot be any green (life).

As Thomas Fuller once said "We never know the worth of water till the well is dry". Fuller means that we will never realize how important water is because it is a matter of survival until we have none left and can't renew it.

So how do we change this? By not wasting water. Turning the tap off every morning while brushing your teeth can save up to 30 gallons of water per day. Please realize now that not wasting water can save our planet before it's too late. Remember, Save every drop!
